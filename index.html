🏓 Table Tennis

Also known as ping pong, or TT

What is it?

Table tennis is popularly known as ping pong. It is an exciting indoor sport. It improves speed, concentration and strategy.

It looks easy to play, but it needs quick reflexes, great skill and regular practice. It is often shortened to TT.

History

The sport began in England in the late 19th century, as an indoor version of lawn tennis. The ITTF was founded in 1926. Table tennis became an Olympic sport in 1988.

People of every age can play it. It grew in popularity and became an international sport.

Story: Manav Thakkar

Manav Thakkar is a great Indian champion. His parents played table tennis at home, and he was inspired by them. He started playing at the age of 5.

His family could not afford every facility for him. He still kept learning and became India's number one player.

The lesson

A person can become good at a game only with interest and dedication. With both, anyone can get better and better.
